We are a non-denominational spiritual ministry, founded October 15, 2024. Qigong, breath, heat, clean food, study, and service are how we honor the life we were given.

We exist to help midwife a new planetary civilization rooted in divine law, right relationship, and embodied sovereignty. That work begins in one body and one home, then spreads through community.
