Questions newcomers ask

Is Bioharmony Collective a church?

Yes. It is a 508(c)(1)(a) Private Ministerial Association: an unincorporated, faith-based spiritual ministry.

Do I need to hold a particular religion?

No. The ministry is non-denominational and open to sincere seekers of any background or belief.

Why is it private?

Members associate by choice and by agreement. Teachings and resources are shared within that membership and are held apart from public commerce.

Is this medical care?

No. Our teachings are spiritual and educational. Keep your relationship with the licensed medical professionals you trust.

How do Origin Institute and Origin Temple relate to Bioharmony?

They are allied companions with their own governing documents. Bioharmony members are encouraged to explore both as aligned companions on the path.
